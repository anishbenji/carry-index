# Fact-check: fibres group

Pages checked: nylon, polyester, uhmwpe, graflyte, dcf (`/home/claude/materials/site/design/materials/v1/`).

Method: I opened every cited source once with WebFetch on 23 Oct 2026 and checked every claim that cites it against that one read. Every source loaded, so there are no UNREACHABLE rows. WebFetch returns a model-extracted reading of each page. Where that reading didn't show a specific word the page relies on, the row says so rather than guessing. I recomputed every conversion. On the DCF page I checked each grade's six table values against that grade's own Fabric Finder page.

OK rows are summarised as counts. Every non-OK row is listed.

## 1. Nylon (`nylon.html`)

**OK: about 75 claims.** These include:
- the at-a-glance figures: density 1.14, regain 4.0–4.5% (6,6) and 2.8–5.0% (6), melting points 262 / 220 °C, saturation 8.5% / 9–10%;
- the Carothers/DuPont history and the monomers;
- Carryology's 20% / 15% figures, labelled "maker claim";
- Ascend's 9–9.5 gpd, and SK75's 35.1 cN/dtex = 39.8 g/den;
- the Cordura Classic AJT range of 330–1000D, and the 6,6 bright filament in the Ballistic and Lite sheets;
- the 1000D row from Ennis (plain weave, 34 × 26, PU, DWR, 329 g/m²);
- the 1050D and 1680D rows (2×2 basket, PU back, "typically");
- the Cordura history: 1929 rayon, 1966 move to nylon, 1967 launch, 40% of luggage by 1979;
- INVISTA: owned by Koch since 2004, and the 29 Jul 2025 decision with US$500M+ over five years;
- the Robic names, Hyosung's 1966 start, Robic as nylon 6 (RBTR and Extremtextil), and RBTR's seller claim;
- Blaze 60 in 100D Robic, and the current Talon page not naming Robic;
- the EVERGOODS fabrics, ALPAKA Axoflux and GORUCK GR1;
- the feel, wet, cleaning and repair notes from Bushwalk, Red Oxx, GEAR AID and Cordura;
- RBTR's 500D (all five colours sold out, $26/yd).

**Arithmetic checked, all correct:**
- 6.5 oz/yd² = 220.4 g/m²
- 8 oz/yd² = 271.2 g/m²
- 11.24 oz/yd² = 381.1 g/m²
- 15.8 oz/yd² = 535.7 g/m²
- 12.32 oz/yd² = 417.7 g/m²
- 39.8 ÷ 10.5 ≈ 3.8, which fits "about four times"

## 3. UHMWPE (`uhmwpe.html`)

- Avient's 8-K: 1 Sep 2022, Dyneema brand included;
