# Fact-check: laminates group

Pages checked: `x-pac.html`, `ecopak.html`, `ultra.html` and the `<section id="fabric">` block of `ultra-carry-index-v17.html`.
All sources were opened with WebFetch on 23 Oct 2026. Every cited source loaded, so there are no UNREACHABLE rows. Every lb→N, oz/yd²→g/m², psi→bar and percentage calculation on the three pages was recomputed, and none is wrong.
