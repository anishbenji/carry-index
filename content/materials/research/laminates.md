# Research: X-Pac, Challenge ECOPAK and other non-UHMWPE bag laminates

Research only, not page copy. Pages were opened 23 Oct 2026. "Page date" is the page's `modified_time` metadata where one was visible; otherwise "undated".

Layout, as the coordinator asked:
- **Part A:** X-Pac (Dimension-Polyant), including X-Pac's own Ultra-PE/UHMWPE variants.
- **Part B:** Challenge ECOPAK.
- **Part C:** delamination, shared by both.
- Then open questions and unreachable pages.

Each part follows the brief's sections: Findings / Data table / Deep-dive claims checked / In use.

Conventions:
- **Tear and abrasion:** Dimension-Polyant's (DP) pages print tear in N and lb with **no test method**, and abrasion as "ASTM 3884" with **no wheel, load or end-point**.
- **Waterproofing:** DP pages print "13.8+ bar / 200+ psi" with **no test method**.
- **ECOPAK tear:** Challenge publishes tear in **lb (lbf)**. Newton figures marked "≈" are my own conversions (×4.448).
- **"Ultra-PE":** X-Pac's word for UHMWPE fibre. Extremtextil sells these fabrics as "backpack laminate with UHMWPE". [Extremtextil UX10](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/x-pac-ux10-backpack-laminate-with-uhmwpe-85g-sqm/73027.SW)

# PART A — X-Pac (Dimension-Polyant)

## Findings

### A1. Dimension-Polyant and X-Pac

**The company**
- Dimension-Polyant says it has worked "since 1966". [Dimension-Polyant – Company](https://www.dimension-polyant.com/company/)
- Its 60th-anniversary post (13 Jan 2026) says it was founded in 1966 as "Vereinigte Seidenwebereien AG Krefeld". [Dimension-Polyant – 60th anniversary post](https://www.dimension-polyant.com/2026/01/__trashed/)
- A DP sales manager said in 2022 that DP was founded in 1966 as a maker of laminated sailcloth. [ALPAKA – Interview with Dimension-Polyant (1 Aug 2022)](https://alpakagear.com/blogs/news/interview-with-dimension-polyant)
- **Factories:** Kempen, Germany and Putnam, Connecticut, USA. [Dimension-Polyant – Company](https://www.dimension-polyant.com/company/)
- **Head office:** Speefeld 7, Kempen, run as Sioen Sailcloth Technologies GmbH. [Dimension-Polyant – Company](https://www.dimension-polyant.com/company/)
- **Other offices:** La Rochelle (France), Fareham (UK), Putnam (USA) and Brookvale NSW (Australia). [Dimension-Polyant – Company](https://www.dimension-polyant.com/company/)
- **Ownership:** DP is "part of" Sioen. Sioen Sailcloth Technologies is Sioen Industries' umbrella for DP and Contender Sailcloth. No acquisition date is given. [Dimension-Polyant – Company](https://www.dimension-polyant.com/company/)
- **Milestones from the anniversary post:**
  - 1974: sail material for the Gorch Fock;
  - about 1984: windsurf laminate used at the Los Angeles Olympics;
  - 1987: America's Cup;
  - the post says over 95% of windsurf sails use laminates made in Putnam (maker claim).
  [Dimension-Polyant – 60th anniversary post](https://www.dimension-polyant.com/2026/01/__trashed/)

**X-Pac's origin**
- X-Pac's FAQ (page dated 14 Apr 2026) calls X-Pac purpose-built pack fabric. It says the technology comes from DP's sailcloth division, which dates to 1966. [X-Pac – Care Instructions & FAQs](https://www.x-pac.com/care-instructions-faqs/)
- **Launch year:** no exact year found on any DP or X-Pac page.
  - X-Pac's own 2023 article says the X4 collection came "in the mid-2000's".
  - The same article says X4 grew out of DP's sailcloth laminating process.
  [X-Pac – Getting to know X-Pac X4 fabrics (3 Apr 2023)](https://www.x-pac.com/getting-to-know-x-pac-x4-fabrics/)
- A DP sales manager called X-Pac a direct descendant of sailcloth laminates (2022). [ALPAKA – Interview with Dimension-Polyant](https://alpakagear.com/blogs/news/interview-with-dimension-polyant)
- **Where it is made:** X-Pac is made in Putnam, CT.
  - DP's 2021 RX release came from Putnam.
  - The 2024 X-Pac Tactical release says the fabrics are made in Putnam.
  - Extremtextil lists the country of origin as USA.
  [Textile World – RX collection (17 Nov 2021)](https://www.textileworld.com/textile-world/nonwovens-technical-textiles/2021/11/dimension-polyant-introduces-sustainable-x-pac-fabric-collection/); [Soldier Systems – X-Pac Tactical (25 Sep 2024)](https://soldiersystems.net/2024/09/25/dimension-polyant-launches-x-pac-tactical/)
- **Adhesive:** the FAQ says X-Pac is "laminated under high pressure with our advanced cross-linking sailcloth adhesive system". The adhesive chemistry is not named. [X-Pac – Care Instructions & FAQs](https://www.x-pac.com/care-instructions-faqs/)

### A2. The current X-Pac line (October 2026)

**Families on x-pac.com** (home page dated 10 Apr 2026). [X-Pac home](https://www.x-pac.com/)
- **X3** (3-layer);
- **X4** (4-layer);
- **LS / LiteSkin**;
- **RX** (recycled);
- **hyperTEC** (Ultra-PE);
- plus a separate **X-Pac Tactical** site.

**X3, 3-layer.** [X-Pac – X3 pack fabrics](https://www.x-pac.com/products/x3-pack-fabrics/); [page 2](https://www.x-pac.com/products/x3-pack-fabrics/page/2/)
- Build: face fabric, X-PLY® reinforcement, and polyester film exposed on the inside.
- Listed styles: X21, X42, X50, X51, WX21, X11, X33 CAMOUFLAGE, X50 CAMOUFLAGE, X50 Tactical, RX15, RX30, RX36 and UX10.

**X4, 4-layer.** [X-Pac – X4 pack fabrics](https://www.x-pac.com/products/x4-pack-fabrics/)
- Build: as X3, plus a lightweight taffeta backing, which X-Pac says is for stitch-hold.
- Listed styles: VX21, VX42, VX21 Ocean Edition, VX07 Ripstop, VX21 Ripstop, UVX40 and RVX25.

**The 4th layer**
- On VX21 and VX42 the backer is **50 denier polyester taffeta**. [X-Pac – VX21](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx21/); [X-Pac – VX42](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx42/)

**Film thickness (corrects the deep-dive)**
- **0.5 mil** shiny polyester film: X21, X42, X50, X51, WX21, X11, X33 and X50 Tactical. [X21](https://www.x-pac.com/product/x21/); [X42](https://www.x-pac.com/product/x42/); [X50](https://www.x-pac.com/product/x50/); [X51](https://www.x-pac.com/product/x51/)
- **0.5 mil** polyester film: RX15, RX30, RX36. [RX15](https://www.x-pac.com/product/rx15/); [RX30](https://www.x-pac.com/product/rx30/); [RX36](https://www.x-pac.com/product/rx36/)
- **0.25 mil**: the X4 styles (VX21, VX21 Ciré, VX42, VX07 Ripstop, VX21 Ocean Edition, RVX25, UVX40). [VX21](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx21/); [VX42](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx42/); [RVX25](https://www.x-pac.com/product/rvx25/); [UVX40](https://www.x-pac.com/product/uvx40/)
- Ripstop by the Roll calls the film PET. [RBTR – X-Pac X42](https://ripstopbytheroll.com/products/x-pac-x42)

**X-PLY**
- Most styles: "Black post-consumer recycled polyester X-PLY® at 22°". [X-Pac – X21](https://www.x-pac.com/product/x21/)
- X50 Tactical: 400d aramid X-PLY. [X-Pac – X50 Tactical](https://www.x-pac.com/product/x50-tactical/)
- hyperTEC styles: Ultra-PE X-PLY. [X-Pac – UX10](https://www.x-pac.com/product/ux10/)
- X-Pac's 2023 X4 article gives the angle as 22.5° and says it runs load from hip to opposite shoulder. Product pages say 22°. [X-Pac – Getting to know X4](https://www.x-pac.com/getting-to-know-x-pac-x4-fabrics/)
- Extremtextil gives X-PLY spacing as about 10–18 mm. [Extremtextil – VX21 Ocean Edition](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/x-pac-vx21-ocean-edition-recycling-backpack-laminate-210g-qm/73026.SW)

**"Soft" variants have no film**
- VX21 Soft: listed "without a polyester film layer", described as softer and more compressible, but still rated 13.8+ bar (maker claim). [X-Pac – VX21](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx21/)
- VX07 Ripstop Soft: described as a "soft or silent" laminate, with no film listed. [X-Pac – VX07 Ripstop](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx07-ripstop/)
- VX21 Ripstop (sold as "VX21 Ripstop Soft"): lists a "Polyester and PU waterproof coating" instead of a film. [X-Pac – VX21 Ripstop](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx21-ripstop/)

**VX21 Ciré**
- A 210D face specially conditioned for abrasion resistance and gloss.
- Rated **2000 cycles** (ASTM 3884), against 500 for standard VX21.
[X-Pac – VX21](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx21/)

**VX21 Ocean Edition**
- 210D face of regen™ nylon from discarded fishing nets, with a post-consumer recycled polyester taffeta.
- X-Pac calls it the first 100% ocean-reclaimed nylon laminate (maker claim).
[X-Pac – VX21 Ocean Edition](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx21-ocean-edition/)

**RX line**
- Announced 17 Nov 2021 as RX15, RX30 and RX36.
- Face fabric: 100% post-consumer polyester from US PET bottles.
- Certified "cradle-to-gate climate neutral" (maker claim).
[Textile World – RX collection](https://www.textileworld.com/textile-world/nonwovens-technical-textiles/2021/11/dimension-polyant-introduces-sustainable-x-pac-fabric-collection/)
- DP's own X-Pac page says the RX line launched in 2022. [Dimension-Polyant – X-Pac](https://www.dimension-polyant.com/x-pac/)
- The RX line page says the 0.5 mil film is not described as recycled. [X-Pac – RX line](https://www.x-pac.com/rx-line/)

**RX30 tear: DP contradicts itself**
- RX30 product page (dated 20 May 2026): 84.5 / 79.6 N. [X-Pac – RX30](https://www.x-pac.com/product/rx30/)
- RX line page (dated 4 Nov 2025): 90.3 / 49.4 N. [X-Pac – RX line](https://www.x-pac.com/rx-line/)
- Weight (262 g/m²) and abrasion (2000 cycles) match on both pages.

**LS (LiteSkin®)**
- Non-woven polyester "LiteSkin" face with a DP UvT top-coat, 0.5 mil polyester film, and a woven nylon fabric as the *backing*.
- The number in the name is the **backer** denier (70d, 210d, 420d), not the face.
- LS pages are dated April 2021.
[X-Pac – LS21](https://www.x-pac.com/product/ls21/); [X-Pac – LS42](https://www.x-pac.com/product/ls42/); [X-Pac – LS07](https://www.x-pac.com/product/ls07/)
- The LS listing page calls the range a "direct descendant" of DP's sailcloth division. [X-Pac – LS pack fabrics](https://www.x-pac.com/products/ls-pack-fabrics/)

**Other X3 styles**
- **X11:** 10 oz natural cotton duck face on an X3 laminate. Its meta description says "X10". [X-Pac – X11](https://www.x-pac.com/product/x11/)
- **WX21:** X21 plus a recycled-polyester "warp insert" at 2 EPI, said to reduce stretch and add rip-stop. The "W" is not defined. [X-Pac – WX21](https://www.x-pac.com/product/wx21/)

**X-Pac Tactical**
- Launched 25 Sep 2024 as five fabrics made in Putnam, many Berry-compliant. [Soldier Systems – X-Pac Tactical](https://soldiersystems.net/2024/09/25/dimension-polyant-launches-x-pac-tactical/)
- The Tactical site (dated 4 Jun 2026) now shows X3 and **MANTA**. [X-Pac Tactical home](https://www.x-pactactical.com)
- MANTA comes in two cores: ULTRA CORE and TYRA TEC® CORE. [X-Pac Tactical – MANTA](https://www.x-pactactical.com/products/manta-fabrics/)

### A3. X-Pac naming

**No official decoding found**
- No Dimension-Polyant page I opened defines the letters (V, R, U, W, L/LS, sT, X).
- The X-Pac FAQ explains only X-PLY. [X-Pac – Care Instructions & FAQs](https://www.x-pac.com/care-instructions-faqs/)
- X-Pac's own X4 article does not decode the codes either. [X-Pac – Getting to know X4](https://www.x-pac.com/getting-to-know-x-pac-x4-fabrics/)

**Closest thing to a DP explanation**
- A DP sales manager said the number reflects face denier; VX21 is a 4-layer laminate with a 210D face. [ALPAKA – Interview with Dimension-Polyant](https://alpakagear.com/blogs/news/interview-with-dimension-polyant)
- ALPAKA, a brand partner (not DP), says VX means 4-layer and X means 3-layer. [ALPAKA – What's in a weave](https://alpakagear.com/blogs/news/whats-in-a-weave-your-guide-to-dimension-polyants-xpac-fabrics)

**Pattern on DP's product pages** (my reading, not stated by DP)
- The digits usually equal face denier ÷ 10: X21 = 210D, X42 = 420D, X50 = 500D, X33 = 330D, VX07 = 70D, RVX25 = 250D, RX15 = 150D, RX30 = 300D.
- Exceptions:
  - RX36 is a 300×600D face;
  - X51 is 500×1000D;
  - X11 is 10 oz cotton duck;
  - LS numbers give the *backer* denier;
  - UVX40's "40" matches its 40% Ultra-PE content, not denier.
  [X-Pac – RX36](https://www.x-pac.com/product/rx36/); [X-Pac – X51](https://www.x-pac.com/product/x51/); [X-Pac – X11](https://www.x-pac.com/product/x11/); [X-Pac – LS21](https://www.x-pac.com/product/ls21/); [X-Pac – UVX40](https://www.x-pac.com/product/uvx40/)
- "V" appears on every style with a taffeta backer: VX…, RVX25, UVX40. [X-Pac – X4 pack fabrics](https://www.x-pac.com/products/x4-pack-fabrics/)
- But UX10 sT and UX20 sT also have a taffeta backer and carry no "V". [X-Pac – UX10 sT](https://www.x-pac.com/product/ux10-st/)
- Prefixes as used: R = recycled-polyester face (RX15/30/36, RVX25 polyester), U = Ultra-PE (UX10, UVX40). These meanings are **inferred, not defined by DP**.

## In use — X-Pac

**Cleaning and washing**
- Spot clean with mild soap and water. Do not machine wash or machine dry. *(Maker guidance.)* [X-Pac FAQ](https://www.x-pac.com/care-instructions-faqs/)
- Wipe with a soft cloth and mild soap, rinse, air dry. Extremtextil says machine washing or tumble drying causes delamination. *(Retailer guidance.)* [Extremtextil VX21 Ocean](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/x-pac-vx21-ocean-edition-recycling-backpack-laminate-210g-qm/73026.SW)
- Revelate: hand wash all bags, X-PAC included; non-detergent soap; no scouring pads; no dryer. *(Bag maker guidance.)* [Revelate – Product care](https://www.revelatedesigns.com/site/instructions)
- Topo Designs (2016):
  - "do not attempt to launder"; spot clean only;
  - for mildew, dry-brush, then soak about 2 h in fresh water with 1% chlorine.
  *(Bag maker guidance.)* [Topo Designs – Why we use X-Pac](https://topodesigns.com/blogs/in-the-wild/why-we-use-x-pac-materials-in-our-bags-and-packs)
- EVERGOODS (X-Pac Civic): hand wash only; line dry out of direct sun. *(Bag maker guidance.)* [EVERGOODS Civic Travel Bag](https://evergoods.us/products/civic-travel-bag-x-pac)

**Hand, stiffness, noise**
- DP sells "Soft" versions (VX21 Soft, VX07 Ripstop Soft, LS07 Soft) described as softer, more compressible or "silent". This implies the standard filmed builds are stiffer and noisier. *(Maker description.)* [VX21](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx21/); [VX07 Ripstop](https://www.x-pac.com/product/vx07-ripstop/)
- GORUCK X-Pac GR2/GR3 (May 2026):
  - stiffer than Cordura, which helped the packs stand up;
  - "a bit noisy", easing with use;
  - stiff fabric made the zips harder to use at first.
  *(Reviewer observation.)* [GORUCK X-Pac review](https://notyouraverageamerican.com/goruck-x-pac-review-better-for-travel-than-cordura/)

**Creases, folds, curl**
- Folded shipping leaves **permanent fold marks** (the retailer says these don't affect performance). Order "on the roll" to avoid them. *(Retailer guidance, on every X-Pac page I opened.)* [Extremtextil UX10](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/x-pac-ux10-backpack-laminate-with-uhmwpe-85g-sqm/73027.SW)
- I found **no source** describing creases turning white (search budget ran out).
- LS42 tends to curl when laid flat. Abrasion at edges or floors makes the surface fluffy. *(Retailer guidance.)* [Extremtextil LS42](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/detail/82c72f72042440a88a52000acf0d7c69)

**Scuffing and wear**
- RBTR VX21 customer reviews mention some scuffing from abrasion. *(Owner reports.)* [RBTR – VX21](https://ripstopbytheroll.com/products/x-pac-vx21)
- In 2017, one owner of a VX21/X21 daypack saw the lighter X21 panels' waterproof layer deteriorate. He also saw X-PLY fibres push through the film; the VX21 panels held up. *(Owner report, forum.)* [BPL – X-Pac long term usage](https://backpackinglight.com/forums/topic/x-pac-long-term-usage)

**Water resistance in use**
- Fabric:
  - DP says X-Pac is 100% waterproof to over 200 psi. *(Maker claim, no method.)* [X-Pac FAQ](https://www.x-pac.com/care-instructions-faqs/)
  - Extremtextil quotes a 140,000 mm water column. *(Retailer, no method.)* [Extremtextil UX10](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/x-pac-ux10-backpack-laminate-with-uhmwpe-85g-sqm/73027.SW)
- Seams and zips are the weak points:
  - In hours of rain the fabric beaded water, but the main zip leaked in quarter-sized spots and a bottom seam wicked. *(Reviewer observation.)* [GORUCK X-Pac review](https://notyouraverageamerican.com/goruck-x-pac-review-better-for-travel-than-cordura/)
  - Able Carry calls its X-Pac bags "water resistant, not waterproof". *(Bag maker statement.)* [Able Carry Max EDC](https://ablecarry.com/products/max-edc)
- Seam sealing:
  - X-Pac can be sealed with sticky-backed or heat-activated seam tape. *(Maker guidance.)* [X-Pac FAQ](https://www.x-pac.com/care-instructions-faqs/)
  - Extremtextil suggests UltraTNT tape inside UX10 seams. *(Retailer guidance.)* [Extremtextil UX10](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/x-pac-ux10-backpack-laminate-with-uhmwpe-85g-sqm/73027.SW)
  - Gluing VX42 lap joints with Seam Seal held less well than hoped; the reviewer blamed the slick face. *(Owner report.)* [RBTR – VX42](https://ripstopbytheroll.com/products/x-pac-vx42)

**Sewing and construction**
- RBTR VX21 reviewers say it sews on a home machine up to four layers. *(Owner reports.)* [RBTR – VX21](https://ripstopbytheroll.com/products/x-pac-vx21)
- Back high point-loads (strap anchors, daisy chains) with an extra layer. *(Retailer guidance, UX10/UX10 sT.)* [Extremtextil UX10 sT](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/x-pac-ux10-st-backpack-laminate-with-uhmwpe-116g-sqm/73198)

**DWR / PFAS**
- DP pages say only "DWR".
- RBTR lists "C6 DWR" on X42 and VX42. [RBTR – X42](https://ripstopbytheroll.com/products/x-pac-x42); [RBTR – VX42](https://ripstopbytheroll.com/products/x-pac-vx42)
- Extremtextil lists "C6 impregnation" on X21 RC and VX03, and "FC-free C0" on VX21 Ocean Edition. [Extremtextil VX03](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/x-pac-vx03-backpack-laminate-140g-sqm/71230.SW); [Extremtextil VX21 Ocean](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/x-pac-vx21-ocean-edition-recycling-backpack-laminate-210g-qm/73026.SW)

# PART C — Delamination (shared: X-Pac, ECOPAK and other adhesive laminates)

## Findings

### What it is and why it happens
**Two different failures share the word "delamination"**
- **Laminate delamination:** the bonded face, scrim and film separate.
- **Coating peel:** the PU coating on a coated (not laminated) bag interior peels away.
  - Tom Bihn (6 Feb 2026) uses "delamination" for the second kind.
  - It says the peel is normal with age, worsened by heat, damp storage and harsh soaps.
  - It says it can't re-coat bags.
  [Tom Bihn – Delamination: what it is and what to do](https://www.tombihn.com/blogs/main/delamination-what-it-is-and-what-to-do)
- **Site note:** keep these apart. X-Pac and ECOPAK failures are the first kind.

**How the layers are bonded**
- **X-Pac:** layers pressed at high pressure with a "cross-linking sailcloth adhesive system". The chemistry is not disclosed. [X-Pac FAQ](https://www.x-pac.com/care-instructions-faqs/)
- X-Pac's 2023 article says the adhesive formula has been tweaked for better performance (no detail). [X-Pac – Getting to know X4](https://www.x-pac.com/getting-to-know-x-pac-x4-fabrics/)
- **ECOPAK:** "proprietary, non-solvent-based, no VOCs". [ECOPAK Features PDF](https://static1.squarespace.com/static/62018e9b29f2cd0fc23c3a0c/t/6206d8d1a7394424ce0818e4/1644615893252/2021_EcoPak-Features_v8.pdf)
- **ULTRA:** "laminated with an eco-friendly adhesive" to PET film. [Challenge – UltraWeave](https://www.challenge-outdoor.com/ultra-weave)
- **Challenge, company-wide:** it laminates with a proprietary adhesive free of toxic solvents and VOCs. It says competitors use solvent-based adhesives and drying ovens (maker claim, no names). [Challenge Outdoor – Sustainability](https://www.challenge-outdoor.com/sustainability)

**Same adhesive as ULTRA?**
- **ECOPAK:** same company and same stated adhesive approach (proprietary, solvent-free). But Challenge never says ECOPAK and ULTRA use the identical adhesive. **Likely the same family, unconfirmed.**
- **X-Pac:** a different maker with an undisclosed "cross-linking" system. **No source says it is the same kind as ULTRA's.** Challenge's marketing implies it is solvent-based, but that is Challenge's claim, not DP's.
- Rockywoods lists "Coating: Polyurethane" on EPLX400 and EPLX840. It is unclear whether this refers to the adhesive; Challenge doesn't say. [Rockywoods – EPLX400](https://rockywoods.com/products/eplx400)

**Causes named by sources**
- **Machine washing and tumble drying:** Extremtextil says these cause delamination on X-Pac and ECOPAK; X-Pac forbids both. *(Retailer and maker guidance.)* [Extremtextil – VX03](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/x-pac-vx03-backpack-laminate-140g-sqm/71230.SW); [X-Pac FAQ](https://www.x-pac.com/care-instructions-faqs/)
- **Repeated creasing and folds:** SectionHiker (on Ultra and laminates generally) says "All laminates can delaminate, given enough time and abusive creasing". Edges and crease lines show film wear first; roll or stuff loosely instead of folding on the same line. *(Reviewer.)* [SectionHiker – Ultra fabrics (2025, updated Jun 2026)](https://sectionhiker.com/ultra-fabrics-for-ultralight-backpacking-gear-tents-packs-and-stuff-sacks/)
- **Heat:**
  - Tom Bihn says heat weakens the adhesive bond of PU coatings (hot cars, dryers). [Tom Bihn – Delamination](https://www.tombihn.com/blogs/main/delamination-what-it-is-and-what-to-do)
  - SectionHiker says UHMWPE softens at lower temperatures than nylon (relevant to UX and ULTRA). [SectionHiker – Ultra fabrics](https://sectionhiker.com/ultra-fabrics-for-ultralight-backpacking-gear-tents-packs-and-stuff-sacks/)
  - **No heat limit is published by DP or Challenge.**
- **Hydrolysis:**
  - MSR / Cascade Designs describes hydrolysis breaking down PU coatings and seam tape over time. It is driven by moisture, heat and humidity, and shows as sticky, flaking coatings. [Cascade Designs – Xtreme Shield (2018)](https://www.cascadedesigns.com/blog/xtreme-shield-system-extends-the-life-of-your-tent/)
  - Whether X-Pac or ECOPAK adhesives are PU-based is **not disclosed**, so applying hydrolysis to them is **unconfirmed**.
- **Manufacturing flaws and age:** one owner saw delamination on a brand-new piece of X-Pac (a flaw) and on a 1990s/2000s Mountainsmith pack. He expected both to stay waterproof. *(Owner report, forum, 2017.)* [BPL – X-Pac long term usage](https://backpackinglight.com/forums/topic/x-pac-long-term-usage)
- **Sailcloth parallel** (background only, not bag fabric):
  - Practical Sailor's laminate-sail repair report shows laminate failures starting at stiff edges, hinge lines and stitch lines, and where the sail flogged or snagged.
  - Its testers preferred stitched Dacron tape over Mylar tape and epoxies for repairs.
  [Practical Sailor – Fixing laminate sails part II (2019/2020)](https://www.practical-sailor.com/sails-rigging-deckgear/fixing-laminate-sails-part-ii/)

## In use — care to limit delamination (summary)

**Maker and retailer guidance**
- Spot clean only, with mild soap; never machine wash or tumble dry. *(Maker: X-Pac; retailer: Extremtextil.)* [X-Pac FAQ](https://www.x-pac.com/care-instructions-faqs/); [Extremtextil EPX200](https://www.extremtextil.de/en/ecopak-epx200-recycling-backpack-laminate-200g-sqm/72449)

**Bag maker guidance**
- Line dry out of direct sun. *(EVERGOODS.)* [EVERGOODS](https://evergoods.us/products/civic-travel-bag-x-pac)
- Hand wash only; no dryer; no scouring pads. *(Revelate.)* [Revelate](https://www.revelatedesigns.com/site/instructions)
- Store clean and fully dry; avoid hot spots. *(Tom Bihn, written for PU coatings, applied by analogy.)* [Tom Bihn](https://www.tombihn.com/blogs/main/delamination-what-it-is-and-what-to-do)

**Reviewer advice**
- Avoid folding on the same crease repeatedly.
- Store cool and dark, out of direct sun.
- Keep UHMWPE-faced laminates away from stoves and dashboards.
[SectionHiker](https://sectionhiker.com/ultra-fabrics-for-ultralight-backpacking-gear-tents-packs-and-stuff-sacks/)

**Repair**
- For filmed X-Pac and EPLX: seam-seal or PSA tape on the film side. *(Maker guidance.)* [X-Pac FAQ](https://www.x-pac.com/care-instructions-faqs/); [Challenge FAQ](https://www.challenge-outdoor.com/faq)
- No ECOPAK can be heat-welded. *(Maker guidance.)* [Challenge FAQ](https://www.challenge-outdoor.com/faq)
- I found no maker repair method specifically for re-bonding delaminated bag laminate.
