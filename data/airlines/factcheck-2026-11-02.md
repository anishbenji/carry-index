# Cabin-baggage fact-check (2026-11-02)

An independent check of 16 records in `airlines/airlines.json` against each airline's own `src` page, fetched live on 2026-11-02. WebFetch was tried first. Firecrawl with maxAge 0 was used for Saudia and JAL, where WebFetch failed (server error, robots), and to get exact quotes for IndiGo, Qantas and Finnair.

Every non-null number, the `us`/`no` flags and the notes were checked. Verdicts:

| Airline | Verdict | Detail |
|---|---|---|
| Air India | **WRONG** (one field) | `co.l` is null, but the page publishes a linear limit: "The total dimensions of the bag must not exceed 115 cm / 45 in." These are confirmed: 55×40×20, 7 kg, PI 40×30×20, 3 kg, the under-seat rule and the 90 cm note, and Business/First at 10 kg. https://www.airindia.com/in/en/travel-information/baggage-guidelines/cabin-baggage.html |
| Oman Air | CONFIRMED | "Hand baggage per guest of 7kg and a maximum dimension of linear 115 cm (45 in)." Economy is 1 piece and Business is "2 piece (up to 7 Kg each)". No personal item is mentioned, so `no: true` is supported. |
| SriLankan Airlines | CONFIRMED | "Each piece of hand baggage must weigh no more than 7 kg and measure no more than a total of 115cm (l+h+w)." "One Laptop" and "One lady's hand bag" are free extras with no size or weight given. Business is 2 pieces. |
| Saudia | CONFIRMED | Guest Class: "56 × 45 × 25 cm … Up to 7 kg". Business/First: 12 kg bag plus a 9 kg briefcase. From the USA/Canada: "not exceeding 16 lb in weight and 45 in". The personal item ("coat, tablet, women's handbag") has no size. (Fetched via Firecrawl.) |
| Singapore Airlines | **WRONG** (note) | The note "Premium Economy, Business and First allow more" is wrong for Premium Economy. The page puts Economy and Premium Economy together at "1 piece … Up to 7kg each … should not exceed 115cm"; only Business/First/Suites get 2 pieces. These are confirmed: 115 cm, 7 kg, the PI at "40x30x10cm" and the 80 cm rule. https://www.singaporeair.com/en_UK/us/travel-info/baggage/cabin-baggage/ |
| Japan Airlines | CONFIRMED (one note unsupported) | "W: within 55cm×H: within 40cm×D: within 25cm", "Within 115cm (45in)", "The total weight of both items must not exceed 10kg." The personal item "Must fit under the seat in front of you", so `us: true` holds. The general note about JAL domestic and aircraft under 100 seats does not appear on this international page, so the cited `src` does not back it. (Fetched via Firecrawl.) |
| Qantas | CONFIRMED | International Economy: "1 overhead bag and 1 personal item. Weight: 7kg (15lb) maximum." Bag is 56×36×23. The personal item is "placed under the seat in front" with no size, so `us: true` holds. The domestic note (underseat bag 34×48×23, "14 kg … max of 10kg … per item") is confirmed. |
| Virgin Australia | CONFIRMED | Economy is 56×36×23 cm, "up to 8kg total". The personal item is 45×33×20 with no weight stated. The page shows no separate domestic/international allowance, only differences in liquids rules. For Business/Economy X/Gold+, the two-small-bag option is "14kg combined". |
| Malaysia Airlines | CONFIRMED | "shall not exceed 56cm x 36cm x 23cm = 115cm" goes in the overhead bin. "shall not exceed 25cm x 36cm x 25cm = 86cm" goes under the seat. The personal item is allowed if "total weight remains within the 7kg limit". |
| Finnair | CONFIRMED (with caveat) | Bag is 55×40×23 and small bag is 40×30×15. The weight line reads "maximum combined weight with a small bag 8/12 kg". The fare table labels 8 kg only for Premium Economy Flex and 12 kg for Business Flex. Economy at 8 kg is the natural reading of "8/12", but no Economy row states it. For Superlight, the carry-on is "0 PC", leaving the small bag only. |
| easyJet | **WRONG** (note) | The note "Paid: large cabin bag (Up Front, Extra Legroom or bundles)" is misleading. The page says these seats do not include a large bag on their own. Only a bundle, easyJet Plus or Inclusive Plus does. Quote: "If the system auto-allocates one of these seats, the allowance is 'one small under seat cabin bag'." These are confirmed: 56×45×25 and 45×36×20 (including handles and wheels), 15 kg each. https://www.easyjet.com/en/help/baggage/cabin-bags |
| Delta Airlines | CONFIRMED (minor) | "may not exceed 22" x 14" x 9" (56 cm x 35 cm x 23 cm)" and "must not exceed 45 linear inches (114 cm)". The stored 55.9/35.6/22.9/114.3 are exact conversions from inches, not Delta's own cm figures (56/35/23/114). No weight is published, so `kg: null` is correct. The personal item goes "beneath the seat in front of you" with no size, so `us: true` holds. |
| Air Canada | CONFIRMED | "23 x 40 x 55 cm" and personal item "33 x 43 x 16 cm". "No weight limit applies to carry-on baggage." `kg: inf` is correct for both pieces. |
| Emirates | CONFIRMED | "Carry-on dimensions should not exceed 55 x 38 x 22cm", 7 kg. No Economy personal item is listed, so `no: true` holds. India: 115 cm (L+W+H) for Economy/Premium Economy. Brazil: 10 kg. Business/First briefcase: "may not exceed 45 x 35 x 20cm", 7 kg. |
| IndiGo | **UNVERIFIABLE** (`co.l` = 115 and the notes) | The page gives "1 (one) Hand Baggage weighing a maximum of 7 (seven) kg" and "length -55cm + width- 35cm + height- 25cm". The figure 115 cm is never stated; it is our own sum. The "3 (three) kg" personal article is confirmed, with no size given. The "Same on Lite fares, domestic and international" note is not stated: the page's Lite text covers checked baggage only, and its two policy versions are not labelled domestic or international (both give the same 7 kg and size). https://www.goindigo.in/information/baggage-policy.html |
| Korean Air | CONFIRMED | "55 cm x 40 cm x 20 cm (including handles and wheels)" or "Within 115 cm (45 in) in total". The personal item is "Not exceeding 40 cm x 30 cm x 15 cm". "Not exceeding 10 kg in total". First/Prestige: 2 bags, "Not exceeding 18 kg (40 lb) in total". |

## Items needing action
1. **Air India**: set `co.l` to 115. It is published on the src page.
2. **Singapore Airlines**: fix the general note. Premium Economy has the same allowance as Economy; only Business/First/Suites allow more.
3. **easyJet**: fix the carry-on note. A large cabin bag needs a paid add-on, a bundle, easyJet Plus or Inclusive Plus. An Up Front or Extra Legroom seat alone does not include one.
4. **IndiGo**: 115 cm is not stated on the page. Either drop `l` and the "Also max 115 cm total" note, or label it as derived (55+35+25). The "Same on Lite fares, domestic and international" note is not supported by the page.
5. **Japan Airlines** (minor): the domestic note (aircraft under 100 seats) needs its own JAL domestic source. The international src page does not cover it.
6. **Finnair** (caveat): the page writes the 8 kg Economy limit only as "8/12 kg". No Economy fare row states it.
