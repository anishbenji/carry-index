# ADR-0008: Reviews and articles carry the owner's real name

- **Status:** Accepted (applies from the publish step, milestone 5)
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Source:** `docs/brief/decisions.md`, entry "B8a: Byline"

## Context

Carry Journal is meant to count as the owner's public portfolio work, and the GitHub profile already shows the real name.

## Decision

Published reviews and articles carry his real full name as author, on the page and in the Article and Review structured data.

## Consequences

Published reviews and articles show the owner's real full name as author on the page and in Article and Review structured data, from the publish step (milestone 5). The owner's email address stays private.

## Why

He wants Carry Journal to count as public portfolio work tied to him; his GitHub profile already shows his real name, so a pen name would add little privacy.
