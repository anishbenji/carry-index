# ADR-0010: Launch the UHMWPE backpack section first

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Source:** `docs/brief/decisions.md`, entry "A9: Launch order"

## Context

The UHMWPE backpack section is the most complete content.

## Decision

Launch the UHMWPE backpack section first (listing, bag pages, fabric pages), with the home page as a short introduction to Carry Journal. Other sections follow later.

## Consequences

Release 1 ships the UHMWPE listing, bag pages and fabric pages, with a short Carry Journal home page. Other sections follow later.

## Why

Accepted the recommendation; it is the most complete section and ships soonest.
