# ADR-0013: Photos only with the brand's permission

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Source:** `docs/brief/decisions.md`, entry "A6: Photos at launch"

## Context

Not every brand has answered a request to use its photos.

## Decision

Photos are published only where the brand has granted permission. Bags without permission launch with line drawings; photos are added when permission arrives.

## Consequences

A photo is published only when its brand's permission is recorded as granted; the build refuses others. Bags without permission launch with line drawings.

## Why

Accepted the recommendation.
