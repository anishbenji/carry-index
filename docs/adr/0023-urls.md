# ADR-0023: Public URL scheme

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Source:** `docs/brief/decisions.md`, entry "A3: URLs"

## Context

URLs are hard to change once they rank in search.

## Decision

Keep the planned routes: `/uhmwpe-backpacks` (listing), `/bags/<model>`, `/fabrics/<id>`, `/compare`, `/about`, `/sources`, `/performance`. Written pieces get three homes: `/reviews/<slug>` for reviews, `/articles/<slug>` for articles, and `/journal/<slug>` for blog-style posts and anything that fits neither. A redirect map covers any future rename.

## Consequences

`/uhmwpe-backpacks`, `/bags/<model>`, `/fabrics/<id>`, `/compare`, `/about`, `/sources`, `/performance`; written pieces at `/reviews/<slug>`, `/articles/<slug>` and `/journal/<slug>`. A redirect map covers any future rename. Changing a public URL needs the owner's decision.

## Why

Reviews and articles each get a clear section; the journal holds everything else.
