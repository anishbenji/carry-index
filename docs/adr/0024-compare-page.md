# ADR-0024: Compare page ships after Release 1

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Source:** `docs/brief/decisions.md`, entry "Compare page"

## Context

Comparing two or three bag builds side by side is wanted, but it is first on the Release 1 cut list.

## Decision

The compare page (two or three bag builds side by side) is wanted, and ships after Release 1.

## Consequences

`/compare` is planned and built after Release 1.

## Why

Accepted the recommendation; it is first on the Release 1 cut list.
