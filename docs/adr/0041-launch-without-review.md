# ADR-0041: The public index launches without a first review

- **Status:** Accepted
- **Date:** 2026-11-25
- **Source:** `docs/brief/decisions.md`, entry "B9: Launch without a first review"

## Context

Review pages need the publish step, which is milestone 5.

## Decision

The public index launches on its own gate. Review and article pages arrive when the publish step lands (milestone 5).

## Consequences

Carry Journal launches on its own gate; review and article pages arrive with the publish step.

## Why

Accepted the recommendation.
