- **Status:** Proposed (spike results, 2026-11-26)
- **Amends:** [ADR-0020](../0020-jpeg-xl.md) (N5). ADR-0020 itself is unchanged until the user decides.
- **Source:** the user's answer of 25 Nov 2026 (USER #3 in the director's decision list)

## Result

Deferred to after the gate by the user (USER #3). N5 concerns review photos, which are not in Release 1. The
spike encoded no photos, so there are no numbers.

## Proposed change

Proposed — for the user at the gate: ADR-0020 stays provisional past the gate. The AVIF against JPEG XL test
runs after the gate, before the first large review photos ship. Until then AVIF and WebP are the only formats.
